They provide everything a business needs to set up, manage, and grow its digital store, making it easy to reach customers worldwide and conduct transactions online. The service offers pre-designed templates or custom designs to create attractive and user-friendly online stores. Point of Sale (POS) System

POS service in software refers to a system or solution used to facilitate transactions between a business and customers at the point of sale, typically in a retail store, restaurant, or online platform. It acts as a central point for processing purchases, managing inventory, tracking sales, and integrating various business operations. POS systems help bridge the gap between sales and management, streamline transactions, enhance the customer experience, and provide actionable insights, making them an essential tool for modern businesses.
