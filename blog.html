WE ARE SincA

SincA is a software that gives better visibility across your business operation and reduces business complexities. This software is specially designed to suit all types of business irrespective of its size. This ERP system empowers you with free access to data alerts and monitor work flow from anywhere and any device.

  • What we do?

Sinca is a software that is suitable for most SME's such as distributors, retailer, wholesaler, service company, beauty, electronics, restaurants, clothing and more. Sinca is designed to cater to people who are not tech-savvy, as everything is designed in the simplest form for all to use. It also analyses customers and salesperson for the business. It can also be used through mobile application whether the company is a multi-location or single installation.

Sales Management

Sinca billing software empowers you to efficiently track and manage your sales activities. Sinca billing software enables you to monitor sales pipelines, analyze performance metrics,sales chart analytics and generate sales reports.

Financial Management

With Sinca's financial management capabilities, you can effortlessly handle your organization's financial operations. Sinca simplifies financial processes, providing you with a clear overview of your company's financial health.

Customer Relationship Management

Sinca's CRM feature enables you to build and nurture strong relationships with your customers. You can store customer information, track interactions, manage communication, and analyze customer data to enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.

Purchase Management

Efficiently manage your procurement process with Sinca's purchase management module. You can create purchase orders, track vendor information, manage deliveries, and maintain purchase records.

Inventory Management

Efficient inventory management is crucial for any business, and Sinca billing software provides the tools to achieve it. You can track inventory levels, manage stock movements, set reorder points, and generate inventory reports.

Lead Management

Sinca's lead management feature allows you to effectively capture, track, and nurture leads. You can manage lead sources, assign leads to sales representatives, track lead progress, and analyze conversion rates.

Human Resource Management

Sinca simplifies HR processes by providing a centralized platform to manage employee data, attendance, leave, and payroll. Sinca helps you optimize your human resource management, improving workforce efficiency.

A-Z Reports

Sinca's A-Z reporting feature offers comprehensive insights into various aspects of your business. You can generate detailed reports on Sinca billing software - sales, finances, inventory, HR, and other key areas, allowing you to make informed decisions and identify areas for improvement.
